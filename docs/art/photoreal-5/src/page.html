Effects, photoreal

Photoreal group 5: every effect in the game, plus the Behemoth's warnings. Each card shows the effect as the game draws it now, then the takes, on the same night street: big, at game size, and as stills at the key moments. Pick by code (for example MZ-A), or tell me what to mix.

One idea for the whole group: every effect is light or matter, and the street keeps the mark.

Light (muzzle flash, fire, blasts, sparks, acid glow, the boss warnings) is emitted: a white-hot core that cools to its colour, a short pool of light on the ground, gone first. Matter (blood, bits, smoke, dust, gas, acid, rain) is lit by the street's one light from the upper left, throws a soft shadow, flies away from its cause, falls and settles; only acid and infection glow. The mark (scorch, blood, puddle, crater, cracks) is what stays, matte and dark. One clock per event: light first, matter next, smoke and dust after, the mark stays. Red = danger, amber = your window, yellow-green = acid / gas; blood is dark red-brown, never the warning red.
