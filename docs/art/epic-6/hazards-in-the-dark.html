Acid and gas in a blackout

One street at night, drawn from the game's own acid puddle and Bloater gas looks. Your survivor stands in the centre with a flashlight pointing right. There is acid in the beam and acid in the dark on the left; a gas cloud sits in the dark just below you. "Under the dark" is how 6.3 is built today. The other options draw acid and gas above the darkness at that strength. Inside the light, they always look the same.

What to look for

  • Under the dark: the gas below you and the acid on the left are invisible until a beam touches them. Only the acid in the flashlight shows.
  • Low strengths (10–30 %): you can just make out where the danger is, but the dark still feels dark.
  • My pick, 60 %: clearly readable on a phone and still clearly "in the dark" next to the lit acid in the beam.
  • 100 %: acid and gas look exactly as they do in daylight, glowing in a pitch-black street.
  • Zombie eyes, your survivor and its ring are above the dark in every option (6.3 as built).
