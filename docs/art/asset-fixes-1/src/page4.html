Asset fixes round 4

No more stickers. The boss's marks are now things happening in the street: light, cracks and dust. These replace the skull, the edge marker and the three icons. Each is animated and has to do three jobs: show who it's hunting, where it is when it's off screen (here, off to the right), and what it's about to do. Pick a direction, or mix them (for example W1's light pool with W2's dust at the edge).

W1 · Red light

W1 Hunting you

A red light pool on the asphalt under the player it is after, with dust drifting through it. Off screen to the right, its red glow and light shafts bleed in from that edge, pulsing with its footsteps.

W1 About to slam

The glow at the edge flashes on a quickening beat.

W1 About to charge

The light shafts close into one beam pointing straight at the target.

W1 About to throw

A shadow grows on the ground where the chunk will land, grit falling into it.

W2 · Tremor

W2 Hunting you

The street cracks under the player it is after, faint red inside; pebbles jump and dust puffs with each footstep, and the view shakes a little. Dust rolls in from the edge where it is.

W2 About to slam

Dust shock rings burst in from the edge.

W2 About to charge

Dust streaks race in along the line it will charge.

W2 About to throw

A shadow grows where the chunk will land, grit falling into it.

Reply with W1, W2 or a mix. If neither is right, a word on what you picture helps a lot.
