Early weapon card art

Side-view shop art for the three Epic 1 weapons that still show the generic gun sticker on the 5.13 cards: SMG, Hunting Rifle and Machete. Three code drawings each, drawn with the game's own weaponArt.ts helpers (same parts, textures, light and outlines as G1, R2, AX1, CS1, FT1, FT2 and GL1).

Each option: big side view (muzzle or tip to the right), the real 96 × 44 shop size, and the real C3 evidence-tag card next to today's Shotgun card. Card art is 26 px tall on the card, so it must read on a phone at that size. In-game (top-down) looks are unchanged.

The family picked looks at real shop size

The new art must sit with these (drawn by the game code, 96 × 44).

SMG $200 · common · S1–S3

My recommendation: S1. The straight stick magazine and wire stock give it a silhouette no other card has (R2 has a curved mag and wood, GL1 a drum), and it reads as “small and fast” at card size. S2 would make a good upgrade/skin look.

Hunting Rifle $350 · rare · H1–H3

My recommendation: H1. The scope is the clearest “long range” sign at 26 px, and the walnut stock sits with G1 and R2. H3 (stainless barrel, bipod) would make a good upgrade look.

Machete $150 · common · M1–M3

My recommendation: M1. The bright edge on a black blade reads best at card size and clearly isn’t the Fire Axe (red head). M3 (rusty kukri) would make a good skin.
