Build bar icons, round 3

Three new I1-style icons for the buildings that still show their top-down world art in the bar: floodlight (from FL1), med station (from MS10) and bear trap (from B-T1). Same 44 × 30 box in a 48 × 36 frame, light from the top left, same outlines and palette as the four shipped icons.

New

FL1 floodlight · MS10 med station · B-T1 bear trap

Floodlight: the yellow tripod work light with rubber feet, the halogen head with its grille and lit glass facing you, cable trailing off to the left. Med station: the blue-grey crate with an orange first-aid case (handle, big white cross) and the grey case whose LED panel shows the glowing green cross. Bear trap: round steel jaws lying open with the teeth up, leaf springs to both sides, the round plate in the middle and the chain to a stake.

Notes

Each frame is drawn exactly as the HUD does it: tile 56 px, black at 58 % with a white 1.5 px border, icon frame 48 × 36 centred 22 px down, price bold 14 px at 45 px, count line bold 15 px 4 px above the grid, Ready slid left of the grid. The icons are baked once at 2× (96 × 72) like the game's hud_build atlas, then drawn 1:1.

The bear trap's price is not set yet (Story 5.14 balance); $120 here is a placeholder for the picture only. The floodlight is hidden from the shop until Epic 6 (?shopall=1 shows it); it is in the bar here to judge the icon.

Drawing code: floodlightFL1, medStationMS10, bearTrapBT1 in this page's script, written in the same form as apps/client/src/render/city/buildIcons.ts so they port as-is.
