Build button (hybrid): layout options — Decision BU-1

Designer decision 2026-11-25: in build phases show a Build button plus the last-placed building as a one-tap quick tile; Build opens the full grid as a panel, and picking from it closes the panel. Story 10.10 (_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/10-10-build-button.md). Pick one code (A–D). Every still is a real build-phase screenshot (dev server, DEV_NO_WAVES=1) with the overlay drawn in HTML/CSS and today's tiles cropped from the game, at 568 × 320 (smallest phone) and 844 × 390, dpr 2. Closed = after placing one wood barricade (1/6, ↶ shown); open = a full-unlock account (7 shop types). The dashed ring is the survivor's 48 px clear zone (annotation only).

Today (Story 5.16, as built): the fixed 4 × 2 grid — a new account shows 2 types, Shop always bottom right

A · Corner pair, Shop in the panel

Closed: quick tile + Build in the corner, count above. Open: a 3 × 3 panel over the corner; Close sits on Build's own spot, Shop is the panel's last tile.

  • Clearest edge: only two controls when closed.
  • Shop becomes two taps (Build → Shop).

B · Corner pair + Shop chip Recommended

Closed: quick tile + Build (count as a badge on Build), a round 48 px Shop chip above Build, ↶ above the quick tile. Open: the same 3 × 3 panel as A (Close on Build's spot, Shop inside too).

  • Repeat and Shop both stay one tap; same panel on every phone, clear of the survivor.
  • Three controls closed (still half of today's footprint).

C · Bottom shelf

Closed: Shop chip, quick tile and Build in one row along the bottom. Open: a one-row shelf across the bottom edge (7 types + Shop + Close); Ready hides while it is open.

  • Always far from the survivor and the ghost; one reading row.
  • Covers Ready and the move-stick half while open; 52 px tiles on 568.

D · Thumb arc

Closed: like A. Open: tiles fan round Build in two rings — cheap traps on the inner ring (r 96), the rest and Shop on the outer ring (r 168).

  • Every tile is one thumb sweep; the most-used are closest.
  • New pattern to learn; tops out at 8 tiles; ragged edge.

Open questions for the designer (recommendations in the story, BU-2 … BU-5)

  1. BU-2 Where Shop lives: B's chip above Build (recommended), A's panel tile, or C's chip left of the quick tile.
