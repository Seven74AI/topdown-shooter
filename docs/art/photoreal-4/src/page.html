Everything rethought around one idea: a body that grew too fast for its skin. Muscle masses follow the hunched back (trapezius, deltoids, lats, spine groove). The skin split where it stretches most (shoulder tops, the seams, the flanks, the biceps), and those splits replace the random cracks and wounds. The veins run along the grooves from the neck to the splits they feed and on down the arms. Enraged, veins and splits glow from the same blood and one heartbeat (1.2 s) runs out from the neck; the eyes pulse on the same beat. The clothes are torn where the body burst out, with strain creases toward the shoulders. It rams walls shoulder first, so concrete sits in the front of the shoulders and grit in the knuckles; blood only where the rebar goes in. Per look: BH1 has one split stapled shut by the lab, BH2 is covered in concrete dust, BH3's tumours grew out of the splits, BH4's flesh swells round its fused armour and tears at the rims.
