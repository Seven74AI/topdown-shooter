Round C looks

Code drawings for Stories 5.4, 5.5 and 5.8. Everything in the street is strictly top-down. The strip under each card is real in-game size (48 px/m) with a survivor (0.4 m) and a Walker (0.35 m) for scale.

Flames, blasts and gas use the fire-lab D particles (the 4.12 fire). Each effect stops at its real edge: flame cone 4 m × 35°, grenade blast 2 m, Bloater blast and gas 2.5 m. With reduced motion every card shows one still frame.

Flamethrower 5.4 · FT1–FT3

Side-view shop icon (with the 96 × 44 shop size), and the top-down look in hand. The small light at the nozzle is the pilot flame.

Flame stream 5.4 · FS1–FS3 · cone 4 m × 35°

Live particles from the nozzle: 3.2 s of fire, then a short pause. The bright flame stays inside the real cone. The Walker at the end burns (the 4.12 burning-body flames); the one at the side is outside the cone and doesn’t.

Grenade launcher 5.5 · GL1–GL3

Shop icon and in hand. The strip shows the 40 mm round in flight (it grows and its shadow drifts with the 1.5 m arc) and the landing ring at the exact 2 m blast radius.

Explosion 5.5 · EX1–EX3 · blast radius 2 m

Aim ring, the round, then the blast: flash, fire, smoke, debris and sparks, a scorch mark and a ring on the damage edge. Walkers inside are thrown 0.6 m out and killed (40 damage); the one at the right stands just outside and is untouched.

Bloater 5.8 · BL1–BL5 · body 0.5 m · blast and gas 2.5 m

Large drawing at left. At right the swell pose (1.0 s, grows to 1.35 ×, glowing). The strip plays the real thing at game size: it walks up, swells while the fuse ring fills, bursts, and leaves a gas cloud for 4 s.

Drawn with Canvas 2D only, using the game’s own helpers (part shading, cloth/skin/steel textures) and the 4.12 particle fire. No images, no libraries.
