Round B looks

Code drawings for Stories 5.7, 5.2 and 5.1. Everything in the street is strictly top-down. The strip under each card is real in-game size (48 px/m) with a survivor and a Walker for scale.

Only small things move: the Brute’s wind-up loop and the running chainsaw. With reduced motion they show one still frame.

Side-view shop icon, plus the top-down look in hand. The swing is horizontal, so the head lies flat and shows its shape from above.

Chainsaw 5.2 · CS1–CS3

Upgrade tier marks 5.1 · U1–U3

Each mark on the Shotgun icon at levels I–IV. At III–IV the icon itself switches from G1 pump to G3 tactical (the upgrade-tier look). The story’s HUD leaves level I unmarked; it is shown here for comparison.
