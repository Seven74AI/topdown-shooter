Round D looks

Iteration 2. Floodlight FL1 (base) and FL5 (upgrade) picked; new med stations MS6–MS10; new gear mockups GR6 and GR7; CI4 picked; all three hand grenades picked; molotov still open.

Code drawings for Stories 5.10, 5.11 and 5.12. Everything in the street is strictly top-down. The strip under each card is real in-game size (48 px/m) with a survivor (0.4 m) and a Walker (0.35 m) for scale; the icon strips are real HUD size in CSS px.

Each effect stops at its real edge: floodlight pool 4 m (6 m in a blackout), med-station ring 2 m, molotov fire 3 m, hand-grenade blast 2.5 m. The molotov fire uses the fire-lab D particles (the 4.12 car fire), the grenade the EX2 shockwave. With reduced motion every card shows one still frame.

Floodlight 5.10 · picked FL1 + FL5 · 1 × 1 m · light 4 m (blackout 6 m)

A buildable lamp seen from straight above, casting one warm-white pool of exactly 4 m radius on wet asphalt, with rain glinting in it. The pool is one radial glow drawn with additive blend (the game’s pooled FX_GLOW, max 8): no lightmap, no shadows. In the strip, shots pass over the lamp (not cover) while a Walker is stopped by it (an obstacle). Use the switch under the strip for the 6 m blackout pool.

Med station 5.10 · iteration 2 · MS6–MS10 · 1 × 1 m · heal ring 2 m

Iteration 2: five new directions, none of them the plain white box with a cross that MS1–MS5 shared (collapsed at the bottom). Each sits on a 1 × 1 m footprint and a soft green ring at exactly 2 m from its centre: a body touching the ring heals 5 HP/s. In the strip the survivor inside gets the heal cue (green plus signs rising); the one outside gets nothing.

Gear icons 5.11 · GR1–GR7 · 5 icons per set

Body armor I, Body armor II, Running shoes, Revive kit, Tactical flashlight, in one style per set. New: GR6 (GR4 objects on GR2 tags) and GR7 (GR4 objects on GR5 stickers). Big drawing on top; the strip is the real HUD: the 200 × 16 health bar, the “HP 100 / 100” text and the owned-gear icons beside it at 24 px (the story’s size) and 32 px, then shop buttons with the “Owned” state. No text inside the icons.

Consumable icons 5.12 · picked CI4 · medkit, grenades, molotovs

The same five style families, on the real 64 × 64 px round consumable button (icon 40 px, count badge bottom right) left of the swap button, as under a phone’s right thumb. The last button shows the medkit greyed out at full HP.

Thrown molotov 5.12 · MB1–MB3 · throw 2–8 m · fire 3 m for 4 s

Aim ring, the bottle in flight with its burning rag (it grows and its shadow drifts with the arc), the glass bursting, then 4 s of molotov fire with the fire-lab D particles: fuel-fire flames, black smoke and embers, flames packed on the exact 3 m edge, normal asphalt with only a soft flickering light (no lava pool). The Walker inside burns; the one outside doesn’t. The variants differ in bottle and in how the fire spreads.

Hand grenade 5.12 · HG1–HG3 all picked · blast 2.5 m

A hand grenade in flight (spinning, growing with the arc), a short roll, then the EX2 shockwave and debris from round C at the 2.5 m radius.

Drawn with Canvas 2D only, using the game’s own helpers (part shading, cloth/wood/steel textures), the 4.12 particle fire and the round C EX2 blast. No images, no libraries.
