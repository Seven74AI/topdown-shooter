Fire Lab

Burning car fires drawn live with particles in Canvas 2D: pooled particles, soft sprites made once, additive flames, normal-blend smoke. Top view is a 1.33× close-up; the strip below each is true in-game size (48 px per metre).

Rule in every panel: bright flame (alpha over 50 %) never passes the 2 m damage edge. Each flame particle is shrunk as it nears the edge so its bright core ends on it; the thin ring line marks the edge. Smoke and sparks may drift out, always semi-transparent.
