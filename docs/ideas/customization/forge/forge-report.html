Survivor customization

Forge session, 2026-11-23 · topdown-shooter · designer + roundtable

Outcome

The idea survived as a look-only character customization that ships with accounts (Epic 7): free starter parts, milestone items, and a very rare loot bag found during a wave. The "bonus equipment" half was dropped by the designer.

Locked

  • No bonuses on equipment. The character's look only.
  • Six visible slots (top, headgear, hair, pack, accent, skin) and any colour for top, hair and accent.
  • Slot ring, name and arrows keep the team colour; chosen colours are night-lit in game; no colour warning.
  • 35 starter, 10 milestone, 10 loot items. Cosmetics never cost Supplies.
  • Loot bag goes to whoever grabs it; the chance is per run (1 in 5 recommended), so the number of zombies doesn't matter.
  • Built together with Epic 7; the look travels as a short code sent once on join.

Rejected

  • Permanent stat gear: would leave new friends behind and break Training's caps.
  • Cosmetics bought with Supplies: a hat would compete with unlocking a gun.
  • A fixed muted palette: the designer wants free colours.
  • A device-only first release: the designer prefers one release with accounts.

Weak points that survived

  • Free colours can make a jacket look like another player's ring colour. Mitigated, not removed.
  • At 1 bag in 5 runs, collecting all 10 loot items takes ~200 runs per player. Fine for a surprise; tune in playtests.
  • Per-player body textures are drawn at join; needs the phone FPS check from Epic 5's brief.

Who pressure-tested it

🎲Samus Shepard, game designer. "Permanent stat gear is a no."
🎨Sally, UX designer. "If 150 items are invisible in play, that's a lie we're telling the player."
🏛️Cloud Dragonborn, game architect. "One string per player, zero per-tick cost."
🎮Dex, player voice. "Don't make me choose between the flamethrower and a hat."

Open choices decided (O1–O5 as recommended, no colour hint); the main session adds draft stories 7.8–7.12 with gds-correct-course.
