Epic 7: progression and unlocks

Supplies, unlocks, card deck, Training, accounts, districts. Outcome: hardened. The designer accepted the proposal after changing its goal.

Locked

  • Unlocking everything is not the goal. GDD earnings stay (+10 per wave, +50 Behemoth, +100 extract, +20 per endless wave on extract). Target: first wanted weapon by about run 3, a few favourites by about run 10.
  • Rookie lift: double Supplies until 300 lifetime. A wave counts for downed and out players too.
  • Starter pool gains the Fire Axe and Revive kit. Free unlock order with a "Good next pick". Unlocks can't be turned off.
  • Borrowed card: 1 of 6 cards from a teammate's unlocks you lack, fixed at the draw.
  • Training stays 50 × level, full strength, never scaled to the team.
  • Guest token also in a server cookie, recovery code, merge on login. The host can pick a district anyone in the lobby has.

Rejected, and why

  • +20 per wave and Training at 25 × level: aimed at "everything in about 21 runs", a goal the designer doesn't want.
  • Training scaled down to the team's lowest: the veteran feels robbed.
  • An unlock tree: 14 items is too few to need one.
  • Email password recovery: collects data and needs new infrastructure.
  • Teammates' unlocks never mixing: new friends would never get to try the good weapons.

Cracks that held up under scrutiny

  • The 6-item starter pool froze a new player's shop. Fixed by D4.
  • Safari wipes a site's script storage after 7 days without a visit. Fixed by the server cookie (D9).
  • Unlocking everything is still a long-term goal (about 40 runs). Accepted on purpose; wave pay is the lever if the playtest says it's too slow.
