# Epic 6, forged (2026-11-23): HARDENED

Locked by the designer (all recommended options). Full numbers: `../proposal.md`; stories: `../revised-stories.md`.

- Behemoth hook: crash stun only (charge ends on cover → stunned 2 s, ×1.5 damage). Rejected: back weak spot (harder to read with a turning, threat-targeting boss; kept for endless later).
- Fairness: zones fixed at telegraph start, fill drawn from the server tick, 0.25 m edge grace, no rewind for boss hits, telegraphs never shorten, one attack at a time.
- Events: always 2 per run (waves 3–8, not 5, not back-to-back). Rejected: 1–2 (some runs felt empty), 3 (nearly every wave).
- Event levers: armed car alarm, shootable gas tank, crate shows its weapon. Rejected: events that just happen.
- Endless: +40/wave to 600, then +6 % HP/wave; Behemoth every 5th +25 % HP; events on even waves. Rejected: +40 forever (5-minute waves by wave 20).
- Ruins debris: ambience only. Rejected: damage hazard (new hazard type in the busiest map story).
- Floodlight 6 m in blackout. Rejected: 4 m (barely beyond the personal glow), 8 m (darkness stops mattering).
- Alive cap 110 while the Behemoth lives. Rejected: 150 (telegraphs lost under bodies, worst-case frame cost).
- Open for the playtest: solo HP (0.4 vs 0.3); the bot solo run went down at 65 s.
