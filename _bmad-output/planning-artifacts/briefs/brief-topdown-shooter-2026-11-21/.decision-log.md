# Decision Log — topdown-shooter brief

- 2026-11-21 — Intent: Create. Starting point: 2D top-down multiplayer shooter, mobile web browser, with co-op zombie/wave mode.
- 2026-11-21 — Stakes: passion project for the user and their friends (not a commercial launch). No existing source material.
- 2026-11-21 — Scope focus: co-op wave survival is the first and primary mode. PvP deferred to a later phase (parked, not dropped).
- 2026-11-21 — Constraints: user reports ample time and budget.
- 2026-11-21 — Working mode: Coaching path (user did not object).
- 2026-11-21 — Feel: horde chaos (power fantasy, big crowds) + build-up/strategy (upgrade weapons, equipment, buildings).
- 2026-11-21 — Group: usually 2–4 players. Each player has an account. Players create and join games (lobbies).
- 2026-11-21 — Run structure: endless, play until everyone dies. Persistent progression between runs (roguelite meta-progression).
- 2026-11-21 — Combat scope widened: not only shooting. Players can play melee/short range, mid range, or long range ("action combat", shooter as one style). Open: class-based vs build-based.
- 2026-11-21 — Meta-progression: mix (option c) — mostly unlocks that widen the item/build pool, plus small capped permanent boosts (keeps newcomers viable).
- 2026-11-21 — Build/upgrade timing: between waves only (shop/build phase). Waves are pure combat.
- 2026-11-21 — Controls: twin-stick (left thumb move, right thumb aim/fire). Melee must work under twin-stick.
- 2026-11-21 — No classes: a player's role (melee/mid/ranged) emerges purely from the gear they acquire during the run.
- 2026-11-21 — Inspirations: user has none; asked Claude to prototype visual/gameplay styles to react to.
- 2026-11-21 — Built style-lab.html: playable probe of 4 art directions (Pixel Grit, Neon Vector, Toybox, Lamplight) + melee/mid/long weapons, to choose art direction. Genre references proposed: Boxhead, Dead Ops Arcade, Brotato, Vampire Survivors, Risk of Rain 2.
- 2026-11-21 — Art direction round 1 feedback: Lamplight concept liked, but not as the main look. Lamplight kept as a special level/wave modifier ("blackout waves"). Pixel Grit, Neon Vector, Toybox not chosen; continue exploring new style directions (round 2).
- 2026-11-21 — Built style-lab-r2.html: round 2 styles (Ink Sketch, Comic Pop, Diorama, Duotone) + blackout wave as a modifier over any style (default every 3rd wave).
- 2026-11-21 — Art direction round 2 feedback: none chosen. Tone direction: more gritty, more scary. User asked to explore zombie styles (zombie designs / types). Round 3 probe: gritty/scary styles + zombie bestiary (Walker, Runner, Crawler, Brute, Bloater, Spitter).
- 2026-11-21 — Idea (not decided): blackout could be the first of several wave modifiers (fog, fast zombies, boss wave) to vary runs cheaply.
- 2026-11-21 — Built style-lab-r3.html: gritty/scary styles (Dead City, Grindhouse, Blood Moon, CCTV), humanoid zombies, bestiary of 6 types with distinct combat roles, teammate ground rings for readability.
- 2026-11-21 — ART DIRECTION CHOSEN: Dead City (gritty, realistic, muted, rainy urban). Setting: the game takes place in cities.
- 2026-11-21 — Core idea: the city starts clean and decays as players advance through a run (clean -> panic -> breakdown -> ruins -> collapse). Blackout waves fit the late "power grid fails" stage. Open: does decay also change gameplay (cover, blocked paths, fires)?
- 2026-11-21 — Built dead-city-decay.html: one street decaying over 5 stages (Evening, Panic, Breakdown, Ruins, Collapse), stage advances each wave; stage 5 = power out, blackout every other wave. Open questions sent: pacing, one street vs districts, decay affects gameplay or only looks.
- 2026-11-21 — Decay pacing: ~20 waves from clean city to collapse.
- 2026-11-21 — Map structure: option C — players pick a district at the start of each run; that district decays around them. (Implies several districts over time; one is enough for MVP.)
- 2026-11-21 — Decay affects gameplay, not only visuals (cover from abandoned cars, fires, rubble narrowing paths, power loss -> blackout waves).
- 2026-11-21 — Drafted full brief v0.1 with [ASSUMPTION] tags for Claude's proposals (core fantasy, pillars, loop, MVP, references) pending user review.
- 2026-11-21 — CHANGE (supersedes "~20 waves"): a run is completed after 10 waves (city goes from Evening to Collapse over 10 waves, ~2 waves per decay stage), then the run continues as an endless mode. Reason: shorter runs suit phone sessions (30–45 min was too long).
- 2026-11-21 — Proposed (assumption): at wave 10 players choose to extract (secure rewards) or stay for endless (more rewards, risk losing them).
