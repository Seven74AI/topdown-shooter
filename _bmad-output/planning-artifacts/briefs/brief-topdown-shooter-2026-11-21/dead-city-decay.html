Game Brief · art direction · Dead City

Dead City Decay

The chosen Dead City look, now telling a story over one run. The street starts as a normal evening and falls apart wave by wave: panic, breakdown, ruins, then total collapse, when the power grid dies and blackout waves begin. By default every new wave moves the city one stage further. Tap a stage to hold it, or restart the run to watch the whole fall again. Same street, same cars, every stage.
