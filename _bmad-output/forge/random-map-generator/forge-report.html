Random City: a map generator

Forge session, topdown-shooter · 12 questions · outcome: hardened. A fully generated 70 × 70 m city each run, alongside Downtown.

Locked decisions

  1. A fully generated city every run. Street plan, block sizes and building footprints all come from rules. No hand-made blocks.
  2. Same map format as Downtown (DistrictMap with decay groups and pick N), so the engine, server, prediction and replays don't change.
  3. Built from the run seed in the shared code. Every phone builds the identical city, with no download.
  4. 70 × 70 m to make room for avenues, T-junctions and dead ends. Waves get rebalanced so fights stay as dense as Downtown's.
  5. Decay = generic rules + landmark recipes. Cars move, cars burn, police lines, manholes and windows open; recipes add set pieces.
  6. Spawns, a mix: stage 1 from the city edges; from stage 2, spawns near the group open (about 15–35 m away, never within 6 m).
  7. Lobby: Downtown or Random city. Results shows a city code to replay or share.
  8. Open from the start, no unlock. The District 2 rule is unchanged.
  9. First version: generic city + one recipe, the gas station (kiosk, pumps, stage-2 gas leak). Art drawn by type and any size.

Every city must pass

ANever seal off an area, at any stage or variant
BEvery spawn can reach the players
COpen start area near the middle, buildable in wave 1, never hit by decay
DCover spread out, with no empty 15 × 15 m square
EAt least 2–3 choke points
FNo spawn path that traps zombies
GEvery street at least 3 m wide

A city that fails any check is thrown away, and the generator tries the next seed. A and B already exist as Downtown tests.

Rejected

  • Downtown variants only and cities assembled from hand-made blocks: you want a fully rule-generated city.
  • Hybrid with hand-made blocks in generated lots, and two separate modes: dropped in favour of one rule-based generator.
  • City preview with reroll: extra UI, and it spoils the discovery.
  • Check H (cover visible on the first screen): dropped.

Weak points that survived

  • Waves 1–2 may feel thin on 70 × 70 while spawns are edge-only.
  • Retuning for 3.2× the area: the 150-alive cap gives a third of the density, and pathfinding rebuilds 19,600 cells instead of 6,144.
  • New players may skip Downtown, the hand-made first city.
  • Downtown's art looks up specific objects by name; the generated city needs everything drawn by type and any size.

Who pressure-tested it

🏗️Winston · System Architect (BMad) Keep the contract. If the generator outputs the same DistrictMap, the risk is in the generator, not the engine.
📋John · Product Manager (BMad) Change the shape, keep the area. Density isn't the zombie count, it's how far they spawn.
🎨Sally · UX Designer (BMad) "There's a gas station, it's going to leak" is knowledge players can reuse across runs.
🧟Dario · player, 15 extractions I don't need a new city, I need to not know where the safe corner is. And let me replay the good one on Friday.
🗺️Inès · roguelite level designer Generic decay turns your five stages into a countdown timer. Cities pass every check and still play like an empty car park.
🧱Teo · procedural-tools engineer Spawns near the players mean spawn points everywhere, all reachable and fair. The generator has to guarantee it.
