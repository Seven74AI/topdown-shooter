Controller support on the phone

Play a run with a Bluetooth Xbox or PlayStation controller paired to an Android phone or an iPhone, in the mobile browser. Forged 25 Nov 2026.

What started it

The GDD listed controller support under "Not planned". Changing the target from desktop to a controller paired to the phone made the idea fit the game's phone-first design, and the "Not planned" line has now been removed from the GDD.

Locked decisions

LOCKController-only. Every part of a run can be played without touching the screen; the one exception is typing.
LOCKPhones: Android and iPhone. The controller selects a text field and calls focus(). Typing is on the phone's keyboard. On Android the keyboard should open on its own; on iPhone the field shows a "tap to type" hint.
LOCKHUD prompts. The HUD shows Xbox or PlayStation button icons, detected from gamepad.id, and the touch joysticks hide. The cancel icon follows the controller, because ✕ means place on a PlayStation pad.
LOCKSwitching devices. Taps on menus or text fields don't switch away from the controller. If the controller disconnects mid-run, the in-run menu opens and the survivor stands still.
LOCKAim assist stays on for controller stick aim.

Rejected

REJECTEDHybrid (touch for build, shop and menus): rejected because you want to play with the controller only.
REJECTEDIn-game letter picker or arcade-style name entry: rejected because the phone keyboard is enough.
REJECTEDHelp screen instead of HUD prompts: rejected.

Weak points that survived

RISKKeyboard from a controller press. Chrome treats a controller press as a user gesture, so Android should work. Safari doesn't, so iPhone needs one tap. Neither has been tested on a real phone.
RISKPlacing buildings with a stick. Story 5.23's carry has only been tried with the keyboard, not at stick precision.
RISKStories 7.3 and 7.6 say menuInput.ts does gamepad menu navigation. It has no gamepad code.
