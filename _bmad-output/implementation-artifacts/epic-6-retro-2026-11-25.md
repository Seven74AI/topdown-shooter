## 3. What went well

- **Nothing broke on master.** Every story landed after a main-session full gate on the merged tree (10/10 close-outs record typecheck, lint, test, build and load) [V]. No red master, no merge-only bug reached it (Epic 5 H1 and A10 held).
- **Re-reviews earned their keep.** All 7 re-review mediums were real bugs that fixes had created (an arrow that strobes 170 px a frame, a vote change lost in the last ⅓ s, a live player denied the dawn by an alt-tab). None reached master [V]. Session 8 lesson 1 ("re-review fixes for real") is confirmed as the most valuable rule we have.
- **Reviewers found what the plan didn't.** 6.8's reviewer reproduced a pre-existing livelock (a RUSH Bloater shuttling forever at a 0.5 m barricade gap) that a bot change had masked; it became 6.13. 6.13's reviewer then found the fix's own exploit (a floodlight in the box brings the stall back: H1) [V].

- **Wiring tests appeared.** `tools/checks/fogWiring.test.ts` (6.9, closing 6.2 R1) and `tools/checks/votePollWiring.test.ts` (6.14) are source checks that fail when a GameScene hookup is deleted — the pattern the draft D-2 asked for [V].
