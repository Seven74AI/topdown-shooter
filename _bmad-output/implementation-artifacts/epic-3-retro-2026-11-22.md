## 2. What went well

- **The review loop again caught the serious bugs before `master`.** All 5 high findings were fixed before merge:
  - 3.4 H1: a player could walk through a 0.5 m slot.
  - 3.4 H2: barricades could not close a path at all.
  - 3.5 H1: a 0.5 m gap jammed zombies for good, an exploit.
  - 3.5 re-review: crowds broke walls they could walk around.
  - 3.8 H1: a Runner that the player kept aiming at never attacked.
- **Re-reviews paid for themselves again.** The 3.5 re-review found a **high regression introduced by the fix itself**. The 3.4 re-review found a medium one, the axis fallback. 2.12 round 2 found two medium ones (pickable seqs, lag-burst edge loss). (Sources: 3-4, 3-5, 2-12 review sections; commits `7545c2a`, `27c86d0`, `a14b036`.)
- **Retro A2 held.** The shared `PLAYER_STATE_FIELDS` / `RUN_STATE_FIELDS` lists were built in 3.1 and fail typecheck when a schema field is missing. They covered every new field in Epic 3: wallet, earned, buildReady, slowTicks, resultHeldOut and others. No "field missing under `?lag`" bug came back (Epic 2 C6). (3-1 Completion Notes.)
- **Harness-first decisions.** The A9 netsim case gave a hard number (28.9 %), and the main session turned it into Story 2.12 the same day (`171149c`). The netsim barricade cases show 0 snaps in every case (3.4), which is retro risk R2 closed with evidence.
- **Early bot sims (A10) did their job.** The economy sim from 3.2 and the shop sim from 3.3 gave real tables before 3.10. The five-wave sim in 3.10 showed that the shop decides the run (pillar 2) without moving any revive or price lever.
- **Budgets were set up front and met.** These were the retro R1/R3 decisions:
  - flow-field cadence P = 3, age ≤ 12 ticks;
  - added p99 ≤ 2 ms, now measured on a quiet machine;
  - turret aim derived on the client;
  - building HP sent on change only.
  - When 3.6 broke the bandwidth budget, the review caught it (M2) and the fix was an interest filter, not a cut feature.
- **Merge hygiene (A1) worked.** There were 37 merges with no red merge commit and no follow-up "fix merge" commit (compare `99ff634`/`e017eb9` in Epic 2). Builders wrote "Notes for the merge" in every story.
- **A reusable lag pattern appeared.** "A request is pending until the server's state agrees" (`readyRequest.ts`, 3.1), together with "UI tied to a build or wave closes when the wave number changes", fixed three separate hidden-tab bugs: 3.1 Ready, 3.4 L3 placement mode, 3.7 repair panel.

**What the patterns say:**

1. **Every high finding was spatial (P1), and reviewers found them with fuzz and random layouts:** 150 layouts × 400 ticks in 3.4, 324 enclosures in 3.5. Builders' hand-picked cases did not find them. Epic 4 is almost entirely spatial: map collision, cover, spawn points, decay mutations that move cars and add barriers, and fire zones. Fuzzing must move from the reviewer to the builder (brief line 3).
2. **P2 is still the largest category, even though reviewers mutation-tested (A7).** A7 moved these finds to review time; it did not prevent them. The worst kind is the **dead sim** (3.2/3.3): it passes because nothing happened. A "liveness" assert costs one line and stops this (brief line 9).
3. **P3 and P4 shrank against Epic 2 (20 → ~15), with no high in either.** The A2 key lists and the A6 checklist work. What is left is *rounding* (3.7), *starved ticks* (3.9), *bursts* (2.12) and *request vs truth* (3.1). These are the same root as Epic 2, now in new places. Epic 4 adds the biggest copy yet: the Predictor must apply the same decay mutations to its own map (architecture §decay).

## 7. Risk note: the unplaytested pile

**No human has played the game since Epic 1.** Since then, everything has been judged by tests, netsim harnesses, seeded bots and headless Chromium at ~14 fps. The following is waiting for a person:

- **Epic 1:** the controls playtest (1.12); a speed test on a mid-range Android (1.11).
- **Epic 2:** all online play, on the real VPS (2.2) and over real 4G with 4 phones (2.10). The rewind decision was taken from the harness, not from phones.
- **Epic 3:** every build-phase touch interaction (Ready + Shop, placement mode, tap-to-repair), Runner and Crawler readability at phone size, the shriek cue, whether 5 waves are fun, and the whole economy. The economy rests on a bot assist share that is a known mismatch with the GDD.
- **2.12:** rewound hits under real jitter.

**Why this matters now:** Epic 4 adds a hand-authored map, cover and three decay stages on top of all this. Its last story, **4.10 Milestone 1 playtest, is the first human gate in four epics.** If 4.10 finds a basic problem with controls, netcode feel or economy, the fix spreads across Epics 1–4 at once, and the bot numbers can't predict whether it will. 4.10 also **needs the VPS live** (2.2), which nothing in Epic 4 does. **Decision (main session):** treat 4.10 as non-deferrable. Fold the 1.12, 2.10 and 1.11 checks and the earnings decision into it (A7). Ask the designer to get the VPS live (A6) while Epic 4 is being built, so 4.10 isn't blocked when the code is ready.

## 11. EPIC 4 BRIEF

Paste into every Epic 4 builder **and** reviewer prompt. It replaces the EPIC 3 BRIEF.

```
EPIC 4 BRIEF (from the Epic 3 retro; reviewers: check each line)
1. Own worktree + assigned ports only. Before "done": git status clean; typecheck && lint && test && build on your branch merged with the current work branch.
2. Map, cover, decay and fire are shared data: server, solo, NetSimHost mirror and Predictor load/apply the SAME map and mutations on the same tick (one shared applyDecayStage). List every copy touched (World → schema → syncState → stateView key lists → mirror → Predictor → devThrottle → snapshotWorld).
3. Spatial code gets a builder-written fuzz test: random legal layouts (cover + buildings + mutations + fires) × many ticks; assert no body inside a box, no stall, zombies reach a player. All 5 Epic 3 highs were spatial.
4. Flow field: map cover joins the nav grid under the 3.5 pinch rule (radius ≤ MAX_PATHING_ZOMBIE_RADIUS_M); fire cells are a cost, not a wall; fields rebuild at the build start, never mid-wave.
5. Build-start order: decay mutations → destroy buildings on blocked cells (refund 50 %) → push out / free-spot return players → rebuild fields. Test a player and a building where a car moves.
```
