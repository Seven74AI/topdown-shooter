## 2. Throughput: what the cloud-session workflow did

Epic 5 ran in two orchestration modes. Times are commit times on `master` [V].

| | Session 6: one container | Session 7: cloud-session builders |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Window | 2026-11-23 12:27 → 19:01 (6.6 h) | 19:01 → 01:53, then 11:33 → 14:33 (9.9 h active; the night gap was the designer away) |
| Stories done | 9 Epic 5 (rounds A–C) + 4.12, 4.13 = **11** | **17 builds / 19 keys** (5.10–5.28) |
| Stories per hour | **1.7** (1.4 Epic 5 only) | **1.7** (1.9 keys/h); 2.0 builds/h in the morning block |
| Tests added | 1,397 → ~2,015: ~94/h | ~2,015 → 2,624: ~62/h |
| Box load | 8–16 on 4 cores; tests timed out at 5 s; `testTimeout` raised to 30 s | main container mostly ≤ 3–4 (gates, reviews, browser checks) |
| Kind of story | sim-heavy (weapons, zombies, `explode()`) | mostly client UX (build bar, drag, undo, ghosts) + 3 sim stories (5.10–5.12, 5.13, 5.14) |

- **[I] The cloud sessions did not raise stories per hour; they raised quality per hour.** The same 1.7/h with no timeouts, no false greens from overloaded gates, and every story pushed as soon as clean (rule 4). The bottleneck moved from the CPU to the **main session**: it merged, ran every gate, ran most reviews itself ("fresh agent, main session" in 5.12, 5.19, 5.20, 5.21, 5.23), and waited for builders that never said they were done (§4 O1).
- **Time from the first build commit to done** (review + fixes + re-review + merge): median **40 min** over 25 stories (5.20 excluded: it crossed the night). Fastest: 5.28 12 min, 5.27 13, 5.24 14. Slowest: **5.12 139 min**, 5.10 80, 5.11 61.
- **Review passes per story:** 8 stories 1 pass (no medium), 17 stories 2 passes (review + one re-review), **1 story 3 passes (5.12)**. Average 1.73.
- **Cost signals [R]:** builders **$8–43** per story session, reviewers **$1–4** per review. Reviews are ~10 % of a story's cost and found every medium; they are the cheapest quality in the loop. The expensive builders were the sim-heavy and the merged ones [I: the $43 end matches 5.12/5.13/5.21-sized stories; not checkable here].

## 3. What went well

- **Re-review gate held** (Epic 4 A1). 18 of 18 stories with a medium were re-reviewed before `master`; 5.12's re-review caught a medium the first fix had undone, the fourth epic running in which the second look found something the first fix broke.
- **Beat the rule worked as a net** (Epic 4 A3). Real exploits were found *before* `master`: free healing by taking back a used med station (5.20 M1, $400 back → now 50 %), buying while downed (5.11 G6 → `not_alive` on every buy), a slot too narrow for the Brute to reach (5.7 → designer rule F9, 1.8 m reach), double knockback and tunnelling (5.3), the last trap catch costing no wear (5.14). Three of these became designer rules (F7 melee vs walls, F9, acid blocked by walls).
- **One `explode(world, spec)`** for grenades, Bloaters and mines; the re-entrancy bug (5.5 M1) and the full-queue nesting (5.8 M) were found because two stories hit the same function.
- **Code-drawn art, picked before the build.** Rounds A–D, build-bar icons and card art were all chosen from code proposals; no story was rebuilt for its look. Meshy credits were not needed.
- **Mutation testing is now routine.** Every story lists its mutants; 14 stories had survivors that became stronger tests.
- **Push each story as soon as clean** (rule 4) kept `master` moving: 17 separate pushes in session 7, no round waited for its slowest story.
- **Course correction mid-epic.** The alignment check found 90 stale-text rows (57 high) after two days of fast decisions; `gds-correct-course` fixed them in one batch the same evening, with the held-back rows forwarded to the active builders.
